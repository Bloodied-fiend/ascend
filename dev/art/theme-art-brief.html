Ascend · Theme Vault · Art brief

Five painted places, unlocked by rank

Ascend is a habit tracker styled after a dark "System" game interface. Players unlock five visual themes as they rank up from D to S. Each theme needs one painted background scene. Code adds all the motion on top (smoke, snow, light, particles), so the painting stays still and quiet.

Decisions for Jaime first

Art rule: these get your call before anything is made. Each one has my default.

  • Who paints it. Default: you, with the same tool you used for the boss Bestiary.The prompts below are ready to paste. Send me the images and I cut the layers, the same way the boss art went in. Other options: hand this page to a commissioned artist, or connect Higgsfield in your claude.ai connector settings and I try generating here. I have not checked what that connector can produce.
  • Style. Default: match the Bestiary bosses.Same painterly finish, so the themes and the bosses read as one world.
  • Coverage. Default: a full-screen plate per theme.The landmark sits at the top and the middle stays dark and soft behind the panels. The alternative is a top banner only, which is cheaper but loses the "you're standing in this place" feel.
  • Motion stays in code. Default: yes.Smoke, caustic light, ash, shadow-fire, frost and snow are the shaders already built in the v5 preview, layered over the new paintings.

What every theme delivers

ItemSpec
Scene platePortrait 1080 × 2340 px (19.5:9), sRGB. One finished image per theme. Generate larger if the tool allows; I downscale.
LayersArtist: a layered PSD or PNGs: sky (opaque), landmark/foreground (transparent), light sources on pure black. Generator: the single image is enough; I cut the layers.
WeightAt most 250 KB per theme after WebP export, all layers combined. The five boss paintings total 1.07 MB, so the themes come in about even with them.
Never in the artText, logos, UI, watermarks, people's faces, or anything the code animates (listed per theme).
OriginalityOriginal designs only. Nothing traced from Solo Leveling or any other series; no recognizable characters or creatures.

Where things go on the plate

  • Top 28%: the landmark (moon, throne, jellyfish, peaks). It's the only part the player sees clearly.
  • 28% to 92%: sits behind see-through UI panels. Keep it soft, low in detail and dark (bright on Whiteout). No hard edges or busy texture, or the text on top won't stay readable.
  • Bottom 8%: the ground or floor, a darker band that anchors the scene.
