Ascend. Level up your actual life.

A habit tracker run by the System. Real training raises five Hunter stats, a monthly Re-Assessment keeps them honest, level-ups earn Rest Days, and a missed day assigns a Penalty Quest instead of wiping your progress. You never lose stats or levels.
