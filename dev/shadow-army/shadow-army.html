[ RULES ]
  • ARISE. Every boss you defeat joins your army as a shadow.
  • Each shadow deals 0.4 damage per hour while you're away. A streak adds up to +50% (full bonus at 14 days); meditating with no phone adds +25%.
  • Shadows only fight after a cleared day. Miss your Daily Quest and they kneel, idle.
  • Capped at 35 damage a week. The other 65 HP has to come from your quests, so the boss still pushes your weakest stat.
  • At most 16 hours count per day away, so changing the phone's clock can't farm damage.
